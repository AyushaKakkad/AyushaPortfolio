Business Overview Dashboard

Transforming business data into actionable insights through interactive Power BI reporting.

Project Overview

Developed an end-to-end Business Overview Dashboard in Power BI to provide management with visibility into key business performance indicators. The project involved preparing and transforming data exported from Microsoft Dynamics and Quercus, developing DAX calculations and KPIs, and creating interactive visualisations to support business reporting and decision-making.

My Contributions

  • Organised Excel data exports from Microsoft Dynamics and Quercus, maintained through SharePoint.
  • Prepared and transformed datasets for reporting and analysis.
  • Developed DAX measures and KPIs to track business performance.
  • Built interactive Power BI visualisations covering leads, enrolments, active students, targets, login activity and year-on-year applications.
  • Validated dashboard outputs and collaborated with senior management to review requirements and reporting accuracy.
